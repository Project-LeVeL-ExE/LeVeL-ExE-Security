# Trademark & Branding Policy

"LeVeL-ExE" is used as the public identifier of the upstream project and may be protected by trademark and/or other laws.

## Allowed (no permission needed)

- **Referential use**: you may refer to the project by name (e.g. “based on LeVeL-ExE”).
- **Verbatim redistribution**: you may redistribute unmodified copies under the GPL, keeping copyright/licensing notices intact.
- **Accurate attribution**: you may credit the upstream author and include an upstream link.

## Not allowed without prior written permission

- Using the **LeVeL-ExE** name, logo, or branding to imply **endorsement**, sponsorship, or affiliation.
- Branding your fork as **the** official LeVeL-ExE release.
- Using the logo/branding as primary identity for a derivative distribution or commercial service.

## Recommended for forks

- Rename forks clearly (e.g. “LeVeL-ExE-based blocklist (ForkName)”).
- State prominently that it is a fork and identify what changed.
